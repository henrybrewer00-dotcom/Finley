How it works

From one sentence to a booked meeting

Finley does the research a great SDR would do for every single lead, then hands you emails worth sending.

Describe who you want

"Dental practices in Phoenix opening a second location." Plain English, as specific as you like.

Finley finds them

It searches the web, reads each company's own site, and finds the decision maker and an email they published.

Every lead gets scored

Fit, need, timing and reachability, each with a reason. Only good fits get an email.

You review and send

Researched emails land in your review queue. Approve, tweak or skip, then send from your own inbox.

Best-in-class emails

Emails that sound like you did your homework, because Finley did

FAQ

Questions

Where do the leads come from?

Finley uses Claude's web search to find companies that match your description, then reads each company's own website for the right person and an email address they published. If you add a Hunter key, it can also look up work emails when a site lists none. It never guesses an address.

What does it cost?

Finley itself is free. You pay Anthropic for the AI it uses, at their list prices: well under a cent to find a lead and 1 to 2 cents to research, score and write its email. Claude Max and Team plans include monthly API credits that cover this.

Will the emails sound like a robot?

That's the thing we care about most. Each email follows a proven format, cites only facts Finley actually found, stays short, and passes a quality judge. You can paste your own past emails so it writes in your voice, and you approve every email before it sends.

Is it safe to connect my inbox?

Finley sends one email at a time from your own inbox, with a daily cap, a one-click unsubscribe header and an automatic do-not-contact list for bounces and unsubscribes. Your SMTP password is stored encrypted.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Finley is open source under the AGPL. Clone it, run finley serve, and everything stays on your machine.
